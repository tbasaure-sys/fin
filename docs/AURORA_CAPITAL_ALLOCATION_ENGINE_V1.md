# AURORA Capital Allocation Engine v1

This layer implements section 14 of `valuation_idea.txt`.

It answers:

```text
For each dollar generated by the business, did management allocate it in a way that created owner value?
```

## Function

`buildAuroraCapitalAllocationEngine(input, options)` accepts:

- `capitalAllocationEvents`
- `allocationEvents`
- `capitalAllocation: { events: [...] }`
- a full belief pipeline input containing allocation events

It returns:

- normalized allocation events
- use-of-capital mix
- repurchase economics
- acquisition economics
- organic reinvestment economics
- debt reduction, dividend, cash-build, and unproductive-investment scoring
- capital allocation alpha
- discipline score
- flags and memo
- downstream adjustments for reinvestment, M&A, buybacks, dilution, and balance-sheet flexibility

## Event Types

Supported event types:

- `organic_reinvestment`
- `acquisition`
- `debt_reduction`
- `repurchase`
- `dividend`
- `cash_build`
- `unproductive_investment`

Aliases such as `M&A`, `buyback`, `share repurchase`, `capex`, and `deleverage` are normalized.

## Repurchases

The guide is explicit: buybacks do not automatically create value.

The engine compares:

```text
RepurchaseReturn = (IntrinsicValuePerShare - AveragePurchasePrice) / AveragePurchasePrice
```

It also measures:

- average repurchase price
- estimated intrinsic value per share
- gross shares repurchased
- net shares reduced
- SBC offset share
- leverage before and after
- opportunity cost

Flags include:

- `bought_above_intrinsic_value`
- `large_sbc_offset`
- `leverage_worsened`

## Acquisitions

The engine estimates:

```text
AcquisitionROIC = IncrementalNOPAT / TotalEconomicPurchasePrice
```

`TotalEconomicPurchasePrice` includes:

- cash purchase price
- assumed debt
- stock issued
- earn-outs
- integration costs
- additional required investment

The acquisition is then compared against WACC.

Flags include:

- `roic_below_wacc`
- `material_stock_issuance`
- `high_integration_costs`

## Aggregate Outputs

`summary.capitalAllocationAlpha` is:

```text
Estimated value created / total capital allocated
```

The engine also reports:

- `disciplineScore`
- `buybackDiscipline`
- `acquisitionDiscipline`
- `organicReinvestmentQuality`
- `destructiveCapitalShare`
- allocation mix by use of capital

## Decisions

- `capital_allocation_pending`: no allocation history supplied.
- `capital_allocation_destructive`: capital allocation appears to have destroyed value.
- `capital_allocation_watch`: allocation needs review before underwriting.
- `capital_allocation_usable`: allocation is usable but not clearly alpha-positive.
- `capital_allocation_alpha_positive`: allocation history supports a positive management capital-allocation prior.

## Pipeline Role

In the belief pipeline, destructive allocation history can emit:

```text
capital_allocation_review
```

That state asks the investor to review management's reinvestment, M&A, buyback, and dilution record before underwriting the valuation.

This layer also exposes adjustments:

- `reinvestmentConfidence`
- `mnaExecutionPrior`
- `buybackDisciplinePrior`
- `dilutionRiskAdjustment`
- `balanceSheetFlexibility`

## Why This Layer Matters

AURORA should not assume that high ROIC automatically reaches shareholders.

The economic question is:

```text
Does management convert business economics into owner economics?
```

This engine makes that question observable.
